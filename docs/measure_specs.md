# Quality Measure Specs

These are HEDIS-style educational implementations, not certified NCQA measure logic.

## DM_A1C_CONTROL

Eligible population: patients with diabetes diagnosis codes.

Numerator: eligible patients with a Hemoglobin A1c observation in the last year and value below 9%.

## HTN_BP_CONTROL

Eligible population: patients with hypertension diagnosis codes.

Numerator: eligible patients with systolic blood pressure below 140 and diastolic blood pressure below 90
on the same measurement date within the last year.

## BCS_SCREENING

Eligible population: women ages 50-74.

Numerator: eligible patients with mammography procedure evidence in the last two years.

## COL_SCREENING

Eligible population: adults ages 50-75.

Numerator: eligible patients with colorectal screening evidence in the configured lookback window.

## CIS_COMBO

Eligible population: children age 2 or younger.

Numerator: eligible patients with the required childhood immunization code set.

## STATIN_THERAPY

Eligible population: patients with ASCVD diagnosis codes.

Numerator: eligible patients with statin medication evidence in the last year.
